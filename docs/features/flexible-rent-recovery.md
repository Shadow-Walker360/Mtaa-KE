# Purpose

The Rent Resilience Score™ measures a tenant's ability to recover responsibly from temporary financial hardship.

Unlike traditional credit scoring systems, the Rent Resilience Score does not primarily evaluate wealth or borrowing capacity.

Instead, it evaluates financial responsibility during periods of hardship.

The objective is to recognize responsible behaviour rather than punish temporary setbacks.

---

# Philosophy

Financial hardship is not failure.

Dishonesty is.

A tenant who communicates early, honours repayment agreements, and clears outstanding balances should become more trusted over time.

The Rent Resilience Score rewards responsibility, communication, and consistency.

---

# Why It Exists

Two tenants may both miss one month's rent.

Traditional systems classify both as defaulters.

Mtaa KE introduces context.

Example

Tenant A

- Stops communicating
- Ignores reminders
- Breaks repayment agreements
- Leaves without notice

Tenant B

- Creates a Payment Promise™
- Communicates immediately
- Agrees to a repayment plan
- Pays every installment
- Completes repayment successfully

Both experienced hardship.

Only one demonstrated resilience.

The platform should recognise this difference.

---

# Score Components

The score considers:

- Payment Promise usage
- Repayment completion rate
- Installment punctuality
- Communication consistency
- Historical repayment behaviour
- Number of completed repayment plans
- Number of repayment defaults
- Time required to clear arrears
- Long-term payment consistency
- Landlord feedback after repayment

---

# Positive Indicators

Examples include:

- Early communication before default
- Successful repayment plans
- Consistent installment payments
- Long periods without arrears
- Voluntary early repayments
- Completing repayments ahead of schedule
- Honest financial disclosures

---

# Negative Indicators

Examples include:

- Ignoring reminders
- Repeated broken payment promises
- Multiple repayment defaults
- Fraudulent repayment information
- Long unresolved arrears
- Frequent unexplained missed installments

Negative events reduce the score gradually rather than permanently.

---

# Score Recovery

The Rent Resilience Score is designed to improve over time.

Successful repayment plans gradually restore confidence.

The platform encourages long-term responsible behaviour instead of permanently penalising temporary hardship.

---

---

# Privacy

The detailed scoring model remains proprietary.

Only summary indicators are shared.

Raw calculations remain confidential.

---

# Business Rules

1. The Rent Resilience Score supplements—but never replaces—the Trust Score.
2. The score never automatically approves or rejects repayment plans.
3. Landlords retain final authority over repayment agreements.
4. Successful recoveries gradually improve the score.
5. Historical repayment records are immutable.
6. Score calculations must remain explainable and auditable.
7. Users may request an explanation of the factors affecting their score.

---

Track:

# Final Principle

> **Reliability is measured not only by never falling—but by consistently getting back up.**

The Rent Resilience Score™ exists to reward responsible recovery, strengthen trust, and promote long-term financial accountability throughout the Mtaa KE ecosystem.
