# Analytics Architecture

> *"Data records the past. Analytics explains the present. Intelligence prepares the future."*

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Analytics Architecture of the Mtaa KE platform.

Its purpose is to transform platform data into actionable intelligence that enables better operational, strategic, and automated decision-making.

Analytics serves both humans and artificial intelligence.

The objective is not to collect more data.

The objective is to continuously improve the business.

---

# Vision

Every meaningful event generated by the platform should contribute to organizational knowledge.

Analytics should answer three questions:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What should we do next?

The platform should become progressively smarter through continuous observation and learning.

---

# Philosophy

Analytics is a business capability.

It exists to improve:

- Decisions
- Operations
- Customer experience
- Financial performance
- Platform intelligence

Data without action has little value.

Analytics should always lead to measurable business improvement.

---

# Core Principles

Every analytics capability must satisfy:

- Business First
- Human + AI Collaboration
- Evidence Driven
- Explainable
- Privacy Respecting
- Near Real-Time Where Valuable
- Historically Accurate
- Continuously Improving

Analytics should create clarity.

Never confusion.

# Types of Analytics

The platform supports multiple analytical perspectives.

---

## Operational Analytics

Focus:

Daily business operations.

Examples:

- Active leases
- Pending applications
- Failed payments
- Outstanding maintenance
- Daily revenue

Audience:

Operations teams.

---

## Strategic Analytics

Focus:

Business growth.

Examples:

- Occupancy trends
- Customer retention
- Market expansion
- Revenue growth
- Regional performance

Audience:

Leadership.

---

## Predictive Analytics

Focus:

Future outcomes.

Examples:

- Tenant default probability
- Property vacancy prediction
- Revenue forecasting
- Maintenance forecasting
- Market demand prediction

Predictions support decisions.

They do not replace them.

---

## Diagnostic Analytics

Focus:

Understanding causes.

Examples:

- Why applications decline
- Why tenants leave
- Why listings underperform
- Why payments fail

The goal is root-cause understanding.

---

## Prescriptive Analytics

Focus:

Recommended actions.

Examples:

- Recommended repayment plans
- Suggested rent adjustments
- Best maintenance schedule
- Marketing recommendations
- Risk mitigation strategies

Recommendations should remain explainable.

# Privacy

Analytics should minimize exposure of sensitive information.

Where appropriate:

- Aggregate
- Anonymize
- Mask
- Secure

Business insight should never compromise individual privacy.

---

# Data Quality

Analytics is only as reliable as its underlying data.

The platform should continuously monitor:

- Completeness
- Accuracy
- Consistency
- Freshness
- Validity

Poor-quality data produces poor-quality decisions.

---

Engineers are encouraged to improve analytics by:

- Reducing latency.

# Success Criteria

The Analytics Architecture succeeds when:

- Every significant business event contributes to organizational knowledge.
- Humans and AI collaborate effectively.
- Decisions become increasingly evidence-based.
- Business performance continuously improves.
- Predictions become more accurate over time.
- Analytics remains transparent and explainable.
- Data becomes a durable competitive advantage.
