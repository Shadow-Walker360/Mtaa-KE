# Knowledge Architecture

> *"Knowledge is the only asset that becomes more valuable every time it is used. Mtaa KE preserves its knowledge as an enduring organizational capability that empowers people, intelligence, and the business itself."*

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Knowledge Architecture of the Mtaa KE platform.

Its purpose is to establish how organizational knowledge is created, managed, validated, shared, evolved, and preserved across the platform.

Knowledge Architecture ensures that intelligence is built upon trusted organizational understanding rather than isolated data or temporary technology.

Knowledge is considered a strategic business asset.

---

# Vision

The platform should continuously transform:

Data

↓

Information

↓

Knowledge

↓

Wisdom

↓

Business Value

The objective is not to collect more information.

The objective is to continuously improve organizational understanding.

# Philosophy

Knowledge should be:

- Accurate
- Explainable

- Discoverable

Knowledge should outlive employees.

# Success Criteria

The Knowledge Architecture succeeds when:

- Organizational knowledge survives personnel changes.
- Every domain owns and maintains its knowledge responsibly.
- Humans and AI rely on the same trusted knowledge.
- Knowledge continuously improves through validated experience.
- Historical decisions remain explainable.
- Business rules remain discoverable.
- Organizational understanding becomes a lasting competitive advantage.
