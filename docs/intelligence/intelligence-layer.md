# 🧠 Property Intelligence Layer™

Version: 1.0

# Vision

The Property Intelligence Layer™ combines permanent data, historical knowledge, and live operational intelligence into a unified understanding of every property.

This architecture transforms Mtaa KE from a marketplace into an intelligent real estate platform.

---

# The Three Pillars

## 🧬 Property DNA™

Permanent characteristics.

Rarely changes.

Defines the identity of the property.

---

## 📘 Digital Property Passport™

Historical memory.

Grows throughout the property's lifetime.

Records every significant event.

---

## ❤️ Property Pulse™

Real-time operational state.

Continuously updated.

Reflects the property's current condition.
