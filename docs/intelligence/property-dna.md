# Vision

Every property possesses a unique identity that remains constant throughout its lifetime.

This permanent identity is known as the Property DNA™.

Property DNA describes what a property fundamentally is.

It is independent of:

- ownership
- listings
- tenants
- market prices
- maintenance

These characteristics rarely change.

---

# Philosophy

People inherit DNA.

Buildings inherit design.

Property DNA captures the immutable characteristics of every real estate asset.

---

# Core Objective

Create a permanent structural fingerprint for every property.

This enables:

- Better verification
- AI valuation
- Historical analysis
- Construction intelligence
- Investment insights
