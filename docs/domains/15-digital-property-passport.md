# 🛂 Digital Property Passport™ (DPP)

Version: 1.0

Status: Visionary Feature

Priority: Phase 4+

Owner: Property Intelligence Team

Classification: Strategic Intellectual Property

---

# Vision

Every physical property deserves a permanent digital identity.

The Digital Property Passport™ is the lifelong digital record of a property.

Unlike a listing, which may last weeks or months, the Passport exists for the entire lifetime of the property.

It continuously grows as new verified information is added.

The Passport becomes the property's digital reputation.

---

# Philosophy

People have:

- Birth Certificates
- National IDs
- Passports

Vehicles have:

- Registration Numbers
- Service History

Properties should have the same.

---

# Core Objective

Transform every building into a trusted digital asset.

The Passport should make it possible for anyone—with the correct permissions—to understand a property's history without relying solely on the current owner.

---

# Permanent Property Identity

Every property receives a permanent identifier.

Example

PROPERTY-KE-NRB-000000152

This identifier never changes.

Ownership changes do not create new identities.

Listings do not create new identities.

Renovations do not create new identities.

# Business Rules

A Passport cannot exist without a Property.

A Passport never expires.

Every change is timestamped.

Nothing is permanently deleted.

Corrections append history.

Audit trails are immutable.
