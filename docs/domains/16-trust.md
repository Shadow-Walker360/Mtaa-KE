# 🛡 Trust Domain Specification

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Owner: Trust Intelligence Team

Domain Type: Core Business Domain

---

# Purpose

The Trust Domain evaluates the reliability, credibility, and reputation of every participant within the Mtaa KE ecosystem.

Rather than relying on opinions alone, Trust combines verified platform activity, behavioral history, successful transactions, compliance, and verified reviews into explainable trust indicators.

The Trust Domain is the platform's confidence engine.

---

# Vision

Build Africa's most intelligent and transparent trust platform for real estate.

Trust should be earned through consistent actions rather than claims.

---

# Responsibilities

The Trust Domain owns:

- Trust Profiles
- Reputation Models
- Trust Scores
- Behavioral Analysis
- Risk Indicators
- Trust Badges
- Reputation History
- Trust Events
- Confidence Levels
- Trust Recommendations
- Trust Analytics

The Trust Domain does NOT own:

- Identity Verification
- Payments
- Reviews
- Messaging
- Documents
- Authentication

Those domains provide evidence.

The Trust Domain interprets it.

---

# Core Principles

Trust must be:

- Explainable
- Transparent
- Fair
- Recoverable
- Auditable
- Evidence-Based

Trust is earned over time.

Trust is never purchased.

# Trust Recovery

Trust is recoverable.

Examples

- Successful repayment plans
- Positive lease history
- Verified maintenance improvements
- Consistent communication
- Identity upgrades

The platform encourages improvement rather than permanent punishment.

---

# Risk Indicators

Examples include:

- Repeated payment defaults
- Fraud signals
- Fake reviews
- Lease abandonment
- Frequent disputes
- Document inconsistencies
- Policy violations

Risk indicators trigger review rather than automatic punishment.

---

# Explainability

Every trust decision should be explainable.

Example

Your trust improved because:

- Five consecutive on-time payments
- Verified identity
- Three positive landlord reviews

Your trust decreased because:

- Two missed payment promises
- One unresolved dispute

Users should understand why trust changes.

---

# Privacy

Users should never see another person's confidential behavioral data.

Only summaries and public indicators may be shared.

Internal calculations remain confidential.

---

# Business Rules

1. Trust is evidence-based.
2. Trust is continuously updated.
3. Trust never depends on one event alone.
4. Trust may improve over time.
5. Historical trust events are immutable.
6. Identity and Trust remain separate domains.
7. Trust decisions must be explainable.
8. Manual overrides require audit approval.
